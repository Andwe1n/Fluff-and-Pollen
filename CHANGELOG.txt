v1.1 — MEADOW & MAGIC

VISUALS
Animated bee wings, gentle hovering, banking, and gradual facing changes.
Patrols ease into and out of endpoints instead of turning abruptly.
Every dirt-block obstacle is replaced by a flowering shrub.
Softer sage grass, warm sand paths, muted forest-green UI, honey highlights.
Dash trails, Breeze effects, and a visible Magnet collection radius.

HIVE ATELIER
Seven selectable ability cards with effect descriptions and ownership.
Abilities and Training tabs; current wallet and three visible loadout slots.
Six Level II upgrades that use no extra skill slot.
Clear costs, insufficient-funds feedback, and replacement confirmation.
Descriptions show current upgraded stats. Unequipping keeps ownership.

BUG FIXES
Dash distance and particle decay no longer depend on render frame rate.
Small simulation steps prevent skipping bee contacts during a low-FPS dash.
Dash direction is fixed at activation; knockback prevents starting a dash.
Negative collection amounts cannot remove pollen.
Escape returns correctly from Settings and How to Play.
Invalid save field types are rejected without partial state restoration.
Save write/commit failures are surfaced in the game.
v1.0 saves migrate with a one-time backup.
Power training and re-equipping cannot bypass cooldowns or refill Shield.
The regression harness now returns a failing process status on failed checks.
